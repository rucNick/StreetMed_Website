Our History

Street Medicine at Pitt began as a grassroots initiative by medical students and healthcare professionals who recognized the critical gap in healthcare access for Pittsburgh's homeless population. What started as weekly rounds in downtown Pittsburgh has grown into a comprehensive program involving multiple disciplines and touching hundreds of lives.
